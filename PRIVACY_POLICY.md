# Privacy Policy - Annual & Benefit HRIS App

**Effective Date:** December 6, 2024
**Last Updated:** December 6, 2024

---

## Introduction

Annual & Benefit ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

---

## Information We Collect

### **1. Personal Information**

We collect information that you provide directly to us when using the App:

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, employee ID, phone number
- **Profile Information:** Profile picture, job title, department, manager information
- **Authentication Data:** Login credentials, biometric data (Face ID/Fingerprint - stored locally on device only)
- **Employment Data:** Leave requests, attendance records, overtime requests, salary information
- **Document Uploads:** Photos and documents you upload for leave requests, reimbursements, etc.

### **2. Device Information**

We automatically collect certain information about your device:

- **Device Identifiers:** Device ID, push notification tokens
- **Location Data:** GPS location (only when using attendance check-in feature)
- **Usage Data:** App interactions, feature usage, error logs
- **Device Information:** OS version, device model, app version

### **3. Camera and Photo Library**

- **Camera Access:** Used to take photos for profile pictures and document uploads
- **Photo Library Access:** Used to select photos from your device for profile pictures and documents
- **Note:** We do not access your camera or photos without your explicit permission

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

### **1. Core App Functionality**
- Process leave requests and approvals
- Track attendance and work hours
- Manage employee benefits and payroll
- Send push notifications for important updates

### **2. Authentication & Security**
- Verify your identity and authenticate your account
- Enable biometric login (Face ID/Fingerprint)
- Protect against unauthorized access and fraud

### **3. Communication**
- Send you notifications about leave approvals, announcements, reminders
- Respond to your inquiries and support requests

### **4. Improvement & Analytics**
- Analyze app usage to improve features and user experience
- Debug errors and improve app performance
- Develop new features based on user needs

---

## Data Storage and Security

### **Security Measures**

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

- **Encryption:** All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
- **Secure Storage:** Sensitive data is encrypted at rest
- **Biometric Data:** Face ID/Fingerprint data is stored locally on your device only and never transmitted to our servers
- **Access Control:** Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel only

### **Data Location**

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (PostgreSQL database) with servers located in trusted data centers with enterprise-grade security.

---

## Your Rights and Choices

### **1. Access and Update**
You can access and update your personal information through the app's settings.

### **2. Delete Account**
You can request deletion of your account by contacting your HR administrator or emailing: privacy@annualbenefit.com

### **3. Opt-Out of Notifications**
You can disable push notifications through your device settings or the app settings.

### **4. Location Services**
You can disable location services in your device settings. Note: This will disable attendance check-in features.

### **5. Biometric Authentication**
You can disable Face ID/Fingerprint login in the app settings at any time.

---

## Data Retention

- **Active Accounts:** We retain your data as long as your account is active
- **Deleted Accounts:** Data is deleted within 90 days after account deletion request
- **Legal Requirements:** We may retain certain data longer if required by law
